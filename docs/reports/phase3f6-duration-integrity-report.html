Real risk found (adjacent to the display, not on it): dashboard.services.tsx lines 114 and 161 silently pre-fill/default duration to 60 both for a brand-new service and when editing an existing service whose stored value is null. A beautician could save "60 minutes" without ever deliberately choosing it. Live data shows exactly 2 of 5 services at precisely 60 — the other 3 are clearly deliberate, non-default values. This can't be resolved from stored data alone.

Decision

Per the stated rule, a genuine stored, user-editable, service-scoped field is kept, not removed. This is a dashboard form-default risk, not a public-page fabrication — the page itself never guesses or falls back. Fixing the form's silent default is a separate, more invasive change (no new field, no redesign, out of scope for this audit).

Flag For You
